Privacy policy

Last updated October 20, 2026

About this policy

System Audio Recorder is a macOS app developed by Nathaniel Botwick. This policy covers the app, this support website, and information you choose to send when contacting support.

Recordings stay in your chosen folder

The app captures system audio when you start a recording and saves it as a WAV file on your Mac. The app does not upload recordings or send their contents to the developer. No account is required.

If you choose a folder managed by iCloud Drive or another syncing or backup service, that service may copy your files according to its settings and privacy policy.

Recording permissions

The Mac App Store version requires macOS Screen & System Audio Recording permission to use Apple’s system audio capture API. The app receives and saves audio only. It does not save screenshots or screen video and does not record your microphone.

You can revoke recording permission in System Settings → Privacy & Security. You control folder access by choosing a save folder in the app.

Local settings

The app stores preferences on your Mac, including your recording shortcut, whether you have seen the welcome guide, and access information for your chosen save folder. These settings are used to operate the app and are not sent to the developer.

No advertising or analytics in the app

The app does not include advertising, tracking, third-party analytics, or an automatic crash-reporting service that sends data to the developer. Apple may separately process App Store and diagnostic information under your Apple settings and Apple’s privacy policy.

Support emails

If you email support, the developer receives your email address and any message or attachments you send, and uses them to respond and troubleshoot your request. Email is handled through Gmail and is subject to Google’s privacy policy. Please avoid sending sensitive recordings or unnecessary personal information.

Support correspondence may be retained to handle follow-up questions. You can request deletion by emailing the address below.

This website

This site uses no advertising, analytics scripts, or cookies of its own. It is designed to be hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information, including IP addresses, when serving the site. See GitHub’s privacy statement for details.

Keeping and deleting recordings

Recordings remain in the folder where you saved them until you delete them. You can delete WAV files in Finder. Removing the app does not automatically delete recordings saved in other folders. Any synced or backed-up copies are managed by the service you use.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s data practices change, this page will be updated with a new revision date.
