Make your first recording

Requires macOS 14.2 or later.

  1. Open the menu

    Click the waveform icon in the menu bar at the top of your screen. The app’s recording controls live here.

  2. Allow recording

    In System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen & System Audio Recording, enable System Audio Recorder. Quit and reopen the app if macOS asks you to.

  3. Choose your folder

    Click Change… under Save To to select Downloads or another folder. The app remembers your choice. Open Folder shows the current location.

  4. Record, then stop

    Play your audio and click Record. Click Stop to finish saving, then Show Last Recording to reveal your WAV file in Finder.

Questions & troubleshooting

Why does it ask for screen recording permission?

The Mac App Store version uses Apple’s ScreenCaptureKit, which requires Screen & System Audio Recording permission. The app receives and saves audio only; it does not save screenshots or screen video, and it does not record your microphone. The broader permission name comes from macOS.

I enabled audio-only permission, but recording won’t start.

For the Mac App Store version, enable the app in the Screen & System Audio Recording section. The separate System Audio Recording Only setting is not sufficient for this version. Quit and reopen the app after changing permissions, then try a short recording.

Can I use a keyboard shortcut?

Yes. The default is Control–Option–Command–R to start or stop recording. Open Settings from the app menu, then Shortcuts to customize or disable it. If the shortcut is unavailable, choose another combination that isn’t used by another app.

Where are recordings saved?

Recordings go to the folder shown under Save To. Before you choose a folder, the app uses its own storage on your Mac. Click Open Folder to find it. Choosing a different folder affects future recordings; it does not move existing files.

The app can’t save to my chosen folder.

Check available disk space and make sure any external drive is connected. Click Change… and select the folder again to renew access. If the problem continues, contact support with the exact error message.

What quality and format do I get?

The Mac App Store version saves uncompressed 48 kHz, 24-bit stereo WAV files. There is no lossy encoding. The original audio source still determines the available detail; recording cannot restore quality already lost in a compressed source.

Are there recording limits?

At 48 kHz, audio uses about 17 MB per minute. A recording stops near 3.84 GB, about 3 hours and 42 minutes at that sample rate. Start another recording to continue. Recordings can include system sounds and notifications. Some protected content may not be capturable.

How can I avoid interruptions?

Make a short test recording before an important session. Stop before changing audio devices or closing your Mac’s lid. The app prevents ordinary idle sleep while recording, but forced sleep, power loss, and Force Quit can interrupt saving. Use Stop and let the file finish saving before shutting down.
